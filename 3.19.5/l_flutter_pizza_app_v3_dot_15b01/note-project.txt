what is yield in flutter?

In Flutter, "yield" typically refers to the process of suspending the execution of a function and producing a value. This is commonly used in asynchronous programming, especially with features like async and await.

In Dart, the language used for Flutter development, the "yield" keyword is often used in conjunction with generators. Generators are functions that can generate a sequence of values lazily, on demand, rather than computing them all at once and returning a List.
